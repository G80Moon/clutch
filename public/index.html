Built for Moraine Valley students

Your semester, handled.

Clutch handles the two things every student runs short on: time and money. It pulls your assignments straight from Canvas, tells you the one thing to do next, and teaches you the money stuff nobody else does, with an AI coach that actually knows your week.

Free. Nothing to install. Your planner stays on your device.

How it works

Takes about a minute to set up. Then it just runs your week.

1
Add your classes and what's due

Type it in, or just tell Clutch "psych paper due Friday, about 3 hours." It goes on the list with the right course tag.

2
Clutch picks your next move

Every time you open it, the most urgent thing is at the top with a reason. Ask it to plan your week and study blocks land on the calendar.

3
Lock in, and bring your class

One tap starts a 25-minute session that goes full screen and blocks YouTube and X. Start a group for a class, share the code, and win the week's focus goal together.

Study mode

Twenty-five minutes. Nothing else.

The timer takes over the whole screen so there's nowhere to hide. If you leave the tab, Clutch notices and tells you how long you were gone when you come back.

  • Tied to a real assignment, not a generic timer
  • Quick links to Canvas, MVConnect, Library and Tutoring
  • Optional browser extension that actually blocks distracting sites
Ask Clutch

An AI that reads your planner, not a chatbot.

Ask "what should I do tonight" and it answers with your real assignments, due dates and hours. Ask it to plan your week and it adds the study blocks itself. It also knows where to find help on campus.

  • Adds assignments and study blocks for you
  • Links to the assignment in Canvas under its reply
  • Knows the Tutoring Center rooms and counseling hours
